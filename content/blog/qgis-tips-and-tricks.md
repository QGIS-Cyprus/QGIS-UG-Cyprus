# QGIS Tips & Tricks

A collection of short QGIS how-to videos — plugins, workflows, and practical tricks. A number of them are directly relevant to mapping in Cyprus, including how to pull historical orthophotos from the Department of Lands and Surveys (DLS) portal.

Browse the full [QGIS playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLasF_QN6wZjSCs39UCX5OyVssyaOdYsz3) on YouTube.

## Cyprus-specific: historical orthophotos

{{< youtube 1i4wBGH1fBc >}}

**[How to add Imagery Orthophotos from DLS portal Cyprus (1963 to 2014)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1i4wBGH1fBc)** — using the MapSwipe tool to bring historical Cyprus aerial imagery into QGIS.
