# The QGIS Visual Changelog Series

Every QGIS release brings a wave of new features, and it's not always easy to keep up just by reading the changelog. The **Visual Changelog** series covers every feature release since QGIS 3.16 — a quick, visual walkthrough of what's new, release by release.

The full series is available on YouTube: [QGIS Changelogs playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLasF_QN6wZjRiZhNWCsxDXe2kEk6nwGIt).
