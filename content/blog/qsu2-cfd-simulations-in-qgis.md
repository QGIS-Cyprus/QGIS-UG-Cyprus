# QSU2: Bringing CFD Simulation Setup into QGIS

**QSU2** is an open-source QGIS plugin, developed by the [KIOS Research and Innovation Centre of Excellence](https://www.kios.ucy.ac.cy/) at the University of Cyprus, that streamlines Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) simulation workflows directly inside QGIS.

<img src="/img/qsu2-plugin.jpg" alt="QSU2 plugin listing in the QGIS Plugin Manager" style="max-width: 100%; height: auto; border-radius: 4px;" />

The plugin enables seamless export of `.su2` files — essential for CFD simulations — providing an interface to [SU2](https://su2code.github.io/), the open-source CFD simulation and optimization suite developed at Stanford University. QSU2 simplifies the process of defining boundaries and conditions for fluid dynamics studies, making it easier to go from geographic data to a simulation-ready setup — useful for engineers, researchers, and professionals working on water systems and other infrastructure.

Built by Syed Mafooq Ul Hassan, Marios S. Kyriakou, and Demetrios G. Eliades (KIOS Research and Innovation Centre of Excellence).
