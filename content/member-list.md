## How to Join

- Membership is open to anyone interested in QGIS
- No fees required
- Fill in the [member registration form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1cqeUq18t3mx55WDs-6lXN7Sf4t8cPOI1cRQldjx8Eag/viewform) above, join our [mailing list](mailto:qgis-cyprus@googlegroups.com), or follow us on [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/qgiscyprus/) to get involved

## Member Responsibilities

As a member, you are expected to:
- Follow our [Rules and Guidelines](/rules/)
- Participate constructively
- Support the group's mission
- Contribute when able

## Member Directory

We're putting together a public directory of members who choose to be listed — coming soon. [Register now](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1cqeUq18t3mx55WDs-6lXN7Sf4t8cPOI1cRQldjx8Eag/viewform) to be among the first listed.
